About Marudhara Fashions

Marudhara Fashions is a Surat-based textile manufacturing company specializing in ladies dupattas and scarves. Founded and led by Mr. Kewalchand Palrecha, the company is built on a strong foundation of quality, consistency, and ethical business practices.

We are engaged in the manufacturing and wholesale supply of fashion dupattas crafted to meet the evolving demands of wholesalers and exporters.

Located in Surat, the textile hub of India, we leverage skilled craftsmanship, modern production techniques, and strict quality control at every stage. Our product range includes stylish, durable, and export-ready dupattas and scarves designed for bulk markets.

We focus on large-volume production, repeat orders, and long-term B2B partnerships. With Pan-India supply capability, transparent dealings, and reliable delivery timelines, Marudhara Fashions aims to be a trusted sourcing partner for businesses seeking dependable textile manufacturing solutions.
