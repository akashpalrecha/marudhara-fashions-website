Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2026

At Marudhara Fashions, we value your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard the information you provide when using our website or contacting us.

Information We Collect

When you contact us through our website or inquiry form, we may collect the following information:

  • Name: To address you properly in our communications
  • Mobile Number: To respond to your inquiries and provide business information
  • Company Name (Optional): To understand your business context
  • City (Optional): To better serve regional requirements
  • Email Address (Optional): For written communications and sending product information
  • Message/Inquiry Details (Optional): To understand your specific requirements

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide for the following purposes:

  • To respond to your inquiries and provide requested information about our products
  • To communicate with you regarding wholesale and business opportunities
  • To provide customer support and assistance
  • To improve our products and services based on your feedback
  • To maintain business records and follow up on potential partnerships

Information Sharing and Disclosure

We do NOT share, sell, rent, or trade your personal information, including your phone number, with any third parties.

Your contact information is used exclusively by Marudhara Fashions for legitimate business purposes. We maintain strict confidentiality of all customer and prospective customer information.

The only exception would be if required by law or legal process, such as a court order or government request.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. However, please note that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

WhatsApp Communication

When you contact us via WhatsApp or submit the contact form (which directs to WhatsApp), your information is transmitted through WhatsApp's platform. We recommend reviewing WhatsApp's Privacy Policy for information on how they handle data.

Cookies and Tracking

Our website does not use cookies or tracking technologies to collect personal information. We do not track your browsing behavior or use analytics tools that collect personally identifiable information.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request information about what personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your information from our records
  • Opt-out of future communications at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are directed at business customers. We do not knowingly collect information from individuals under the age of 18.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
